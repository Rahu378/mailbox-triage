Middle-office automation

The shared mailbox, sorted.
Before anyone opens it.

An n8n workflow reads every email that hits the middle-office inbox, has an LLM sort it and pull out the trade ID, CUSIP/ISIN, quantity and settlement date, then opens a Jira ticket prioritised for T+1. Anything that touches bank details goes to a call-back instead. Replies are drafted, never sent.
